# SRD Agent Glossary

> [!TIP]
> Use this file for O(1) lookups of Conditions, Actions, and Core Rules without opening 100kb files.

## Conditions (Brief)
*   **Blinded**: Fail sight checks. Attacks vs you have Adv; your attacks have Disadv.
*   **Charmed**: Can't attack charmer. Charmer has Adv on social checks.
*   **Deafened**: Fail hearing checks.
*   **Exhaustion**: Lvl 1: Disadv on checks. Lvl 2: Speed halved. Lvl 3: Disadv on attacks/saves. Lvl 4: HP Max halved. Lvl 5: Speed 0. Lvl 6: Death. (Note: 5e 2014 vs 2024 rules may vary, this is SRD 5.1 standard).
*   **Frightened**: Disadv on checks/attacks while source visible. Can't move closer.
*   **Grappled**: Speed 0.
*   **Incapacitated**: No Actions/Reactions. Conc broken.
*   **Invisible**: Adv on attacks; attacks vs you have Disadv. Considered "Heavily Obscured".
*   **Paralyzed**: Incapacitated. Speed 0. Auto-fail Str/Dex saves. Attacks vs you have Adv (Auto-crit if within 5ft).
*   **Petrified**: Incapacitated. Weight x10. Resistance to all damage. Immune to Poison/Disease.
*   **Poisoned**: Disadv on attacks/checks.
*   **Prone**: Attacks vs you have Adv (within 5ft) or Disadv (outside 5ft). Your attacks have Disadv. Crawl at half speed.
*   **Restrained**: Speed 0. Attacks vs you have Adv; yours have Disadv. Disadv on Dex saves.
*   **Stunned**: Incapacitated. Fail Str/Dex saves. Attacks vs you have Adv.
*   **Unconscious**: Incapacitated + Prone. Drop items. Auto-fail Str/Dex saves. Attacks vs you have Adv (Auto-crit 5ft).

## Actions in Combat
*   **Attack**: Melee/Ranged attack.
*   **Cast a Spell**: Cast time of 1 Action.
*   **Dash**: Gain extra movement equal to Speed.
*   **Disengage**: Movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks.
*   **Dodge**: Attacks vs you have Disadv (if you see attacker). You have Adv on Dex saves.
*   **Help**: Give Ally Adv on next check/attack.
*   **Hide**: Stealth check vs Perception. Needs Cover/Obscurement.
*   **Ready**: Hold action for Trigger. Uses Reaction. Conc required for spells.
*   **Search**: Wisdom (Perception/Investigation) check.
*   **Use an Object**: Interact with object requiring action.

## Ability Scores
*   **Strength**: Athletics. Carrying (15x Str).
*   **Dexterity**: Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, Stealth. AC, Init, Reflex.
*   **Constitution**: HP, Conc saves. No skills.
*   **Intelligence**: Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, Religion.
*   **Wisdom**: Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Perception, Survival.
*   **Charisma**: Deception, Intimidation, Performance, Persuasion.

## Cover
*   **Half Cover**: +2 AC/Dex Saves.
*   **Three-Quarters**: +5 AC/Dex Saves.
*   **Total**: Can't be targeted directly.

## Light & Vision
*   **Lightly Obscured** (Dim Light): Disadv on Perception.
*   **Heavily Obscured** (Darkness): Blinded condition.

## Resting
*   **Short Rest**: 1 hour. Spend Hit Dice (add Con) to regain HP. Regain Warlock slots, Monk Ki, etc.
*   **Long Rest**: 8 hours (6 sleep). Regain all HP, 1/2 Hit Dice. Regain spell slots. Reduce Exhaustion by 1.

## Currency
*   **CP**: 1/100 GP
*   **SP**: 1/10 GP
*   **EP**: 1/2 GP
*   **GP**: 1 GP
*   **PP**: 10 GP
