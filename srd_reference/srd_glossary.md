## Actions in Combat
*   **Attack**: When you take the Attack action, you can make one attack roll with a weapon or an Unarmed Strike. **Equipping and Unequipping Weapons.
*   **Dash**: When you take the Dash action, you gain extra movement for the current turn. The increase equals your Speed after applying any modifiers.
*   **Disengage**: If you take the Disengage action, your movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks for the rest of the current turn.
*   **Dodge**: If you take the Dodge action, you gain the following benefits: until the start of your next turn, any attack roll made against you has Disadvantage if you can see the attacker, and you make Dexterity...
*   **Help**: When you take the Help action, you do one of the following. **Assist an Ability Check.
*   **Hide**: With the Hide action, you try to hide yourself.
*   **Influence**: With the Influence action, you urge a monster to do something. Describe or roleplay how you're communicating with the monster. Are you trying to deceive, intimidate, amuse, or gently persuade?
*   **Magic**: When you take the Magic action, you cast a spell that has a casting time of an action or use a feature or magic item that requires a Magic action to be activated.
*   **Ready**: You take the Ready action to wait for a particular circumstance before you act. To do so, you take this action on your turn, which lets you act by taking a Reaction before the start of your next turn.
*   **Search**: When you take the Search action, you make a Wisdom check to discern something that isn't obvious.
*   **Study**: When you take the Study action, you make an Intelligence check to study your memory, a book, a clue, or another source of knowledge and call to mind an important piece of information about it.
*   **Utilize**: You normally interact with an object while doing something else, such as when you draw a sword as part of the Attack action. When an object requires an action for its use, you take the Utilize action.

## Ability Scores
*   **Strength**: Athletics skill. Melee attacks, carrying capacity (15x Str score).
*   **Dexterity**: Acrobatics, Sleight of Hand, Stealth skills. AC, Initiative, Ranged attacks.
*   **Constitution**: Hit Points, Concentration saves. No associated skills.
*   **Intelligence**: Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, Religion skills.
*   **Wisdom**: Animal Handling, Insight, Medicine, Perception, Survival skills.
*   **Charisma**: Deception, Intimidation, Performance, Persuasion skills.

## Cover
*   **Half Cover**: +2 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws.
*   **Three-Quarters Cover**: +5 bonus to AC and Dexterity saving throws.
*   **Total Cover**: Cannot be targeted directly by attacks or spells.

## Light & Vision
*   **Bright Light**: Normal vision for most creatures.
*   **Dim Light**: Lightly obscured. Disadvantage on Perception checks.
*   **Darkness**: Heavily obscured. Creatures are effectively Blinded.
*   **Lightly Obscured**: Dim light, patchy fog. Disadvantage on Perception.
*   **Heavily Obscured**: Darkness, opaque fog. Blinded condition.

## Resting
*   **Short Rest**: 1+ hours of downtime. Spend Hit Dice to regain HP. Some class features recharge.
*   **Long Rest**: 8 hours of sleep/light activity. Regain all HP and Hit Dice (up to half). Most abilities recharge.
